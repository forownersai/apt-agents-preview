Before your agent calls

A few minutes with your agent makes a better list.

Your answers get us started. The conversation is what turns them into a shortlist worth touring, so please pick up or reply when your agent reaches out.

  • Matches you’d actually rent

    Your agent asks about the things a form can’t capture: Where you want to live, the commute you want to avoid, and any desired amenities or deal-breakers.

  • Prices for your move date

    Rents and specials change often. Knowing more about your move-in window and lease preferences lets your agent price the units that will actually be open when you need them.

  • Fewer tours, faster decision

    The more your agent knows, the better your recommendations. You spend your time on the places that are real contenders.

How we’re paid, and how you get paid

Two steps that earn you up to $250.

Our service is free because apartment communities pay us when someone we refer signs a lease. These two steps make sure they know you came from us, and they qualify you for your cash reward.

1

On Every Guest Card

When you visit in person or schedule a tour online, tell the leasing team:

“I learned about your property from your agent’s name with Apartment Agents.”

Make sure Apartment Agents is listed as your referral source on the guest card before or during your first tour.

2

On Your Rental Application

List your agent’s name with Apartment Agents under “How did you hear about us?” or “Were you referred to us?” When applying online, select Locator service, realtor, broker, or rental agency.

Important: This is how we’re paid for helping you, and it qualifies you for your cash reward. Please do not list another referral source, such as the property’s website, Apartments.com, Google, Zillow, a friend, or “drove by”.
