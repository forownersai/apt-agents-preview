Straight answers

The questions everyone asks.

Short versions, no fine-print games.

You can, and plenty of people do. The rent is identical either way, because communities don't give you a better price for going direct. So doing it alone means the same apartment, at the same price, minus up to $250. You'd also be doing the slow part yourself: scrolling listing sites, sorting out the fake listings, and leaving voicemails at leasing offices that never get returned. Then guessing at everything a listing never tells you.
There's no catch, but there is a reason. Communities set aside a marketing budget to fill their units and pay us a referral fee only when someone we refer signs a lease. You never pay us anything, and your rent isn't a dollar higher for using us.
Straight answer: we only work with communities that pay a referral fee, and that's what makes this free for you. But we're not paid unless you actually sign a lease, so sending you somewhere that doesn't fit what you asked for earns us nothing. A large share of our business comes from repeat clients and referrals, so a bad recommendation costs us far more than a bigger fee is ever worth.
