## **Brand overview**

Apartment Agents is a Texas based apartment locating service that helps renters in Dallas–Fort Worth find their best apartment options and the best deals available. The service is free to renters and is positioned as “better than free” because clients typically save more money using Apartment Agents than they would searching on their own.

The brand should present as expert, deal-focused, and renter-first: a team that knows the market, finds the best pricing and specials, and helps cover up to $200 of their moving costs by paying up to $200 of their move with a moving company of their choice or giving them extra cash on top of any discounts the property offers so renters come out ahead.

---

## **Brand foundation**

## **Brand promise**

Apartment Agents is the better‑than‑free way to find your next apartment: matching you with your best options, securing the best deals, and giving you up to a $200 free move or extra cash after you lease.

## **Value proposition**

* Match renters with their best apartment options based on budget, location, timing, and preferences.  
* We’re local experts who match you with your best options and properties with the best deals.  
* Deliver a free service that often saves renters more than they could save alone.  
* Add up to a $200 free move or extra cash on top of property specials.

## **Brand pillars**

1. Best options, not just listings  
   Personalized matches instead of generic link dumps. Every recommendation is filtered by price, timing, location, and renter preferences.  
2. Best deals on those options  
   Full visibility into property specials, concessions, and incentives so renters don’t leave money on the table.  
3. Better than free  
   The service is free to renters, and they typically save more by finding the best deals and providing up to a $200 free move or cash bonus on top of any lease concessions the properties offer.  
4. You choose the mover, we pay  
   Renters pick the moving company they actually want; Apartment Agents covers up to $200 of the move and also offers exclusive moving discounts.  
5. Fast, expert guidance  
   Local apartment specialists who know the inventory, pricing patterns, and how to find the best deal.

---

## **Audience**

* Primary: Renters in Dallas–Fort Worth looking for a new apartment in the next 30–90 days.  
* Secondary:  
  * Relocating professionals primarily in the 25-35 age demographic.  
  * Renters who are looking for new or recently built apartments that are class A properties  
  * Renters comparing specials and incentives across multiple properties

---

## **Brand personality**

* Deal‑savvy: Clearly focused on value, pricing, and specials.  
* Local and knowledgeable: Feels like talking to a plugged‑in local, not a national portal.  
* Direct and clear: No fluff; straight answers and clear next steps.  
* Supportive: Reduces stress and decision fatigue around moving.  
* Efficient: Respectful of the renter’s time with focused, high‑quality options.

## **Voice and tone**

## **Voice principles**

* Clear and direct: Say exactly what the benefit is and how it works.  
* Value‑driven: Always connect features back to savings or advantages.  
* Transparent: Explain offers and rewards plainly; avoid fine‑print language.  
* Helpful: Give renters a sense of control and confidence in their decision.

## **Tone by context**

* Homepage / hero: Confident, benefit‑heavy, simple.  
* Pricing/specials content: Precise, numbers‑oriented, comparison‑friendly.  
* Email/SMS follow‑up: Personal, punchy, next‑step focused.  
* Rewards/move offer pages: Clear, concrete, with simple explanations and examples.

## **Visual identity**

**Brand Colors**  
Apartment Agents uses a core two-color identity supported by three promotional accent colors drawn from the Dallas apartments experience.

**Core colors**

* Dark Blue: \#0F1338  
* Purple: \#9DAAF2  
  Support colors  
* Coral: \#F5736C  
* Green: \#AECD81  
* Yellow: \#FFD67B  
  Usage guidance  
* Use Dark Blue as the primary foundation color for brand trust, readability, and structure.  
* Use Purple as the main accent for interactive elements and modern brand personality.  
* Use Coral, Green, and Yellow as secondary support colors for promotions, savings, specials, incentives, and visual variety in marketing materials.  
* Do not let the support colors overpower the core brand system; they should enhance campaigns and content, not replace the main identity.

## **Best practical setup**

For the website and marketing:

* 80% core colors: Dark Blue, white/light neutrals, Purple.  
* 20% support colors: Coral, Green, Yellow for badges, cards, category tags, promos, and savings graphics.

## **Content guidelines**

## **Preferred language**

* “Free apartment locating that’s better than free.”  
* “We match you with your best apartment options.”  
* “Get the best deals and all the specials.”  
* “Up to a $200 free move or extra cash.”

## **Avoid**

* Long, dense paragraphs without clear benefit bullets.  
* Generic “we care about you” statements that don’t mention matching, deals, or the move offer.
