Who We Are

Built by Dallas locals, for Dallas renters

Apartment Agents was founded in Dallas with one straightforward idea: renters deserve better than spending hours sifting through listings, calling leasing offices, and guessing which apartments actually have the best deals.

Our licensed agents focus 100% on apartment rentals, not home sales. Every bit of our expertise goes into getting you the right place at the right price. We live and breathe the DFW rental market so you don't have to.

Over two decades later, we've helped more than 200,000 renters across Dallas, Uptown, Frisco, Plano, Fort Worth, and every corner of the Metroplex find their next home faster, smarter, and with more money in their pocket.
