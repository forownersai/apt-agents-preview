Our story

Twenty-three years in the same market.

Apartment Agents opened in Dallas in 2003. The market was slower then. Rents held for months at a stretch, and if you wanted to know what a one bedroom in Uptown went for, you called the leasing office and somebody picked up the phone.

That is not the market anymore. Pricing is set by revenue software now and it can move week to week on the same floor plan. There are more listing sites than anyone can keep up with, and plenty of what sits on them is stale or was never real. Leave a message at a busy leasing office today and there is a decent chance nobody calls you back.

What has not changed is the part that is genuinely hard to look up. Which communities are running a real special this month and which ones only say so. Which ones residents complain about for maintenance. What a block actually feels like at ten on a Friday night. None of that is on a listing site. It sits with people who work this market every day, and that is the whole reason a company like ours still exists.

We have helped more than 200,000 DFW renters since 2003. A good share of our business every year is people who moved with us before, or a friend they sent.

We are still a small team. Two agents take new searches full time, one works a book of repeat clients she has had for years, and the owner is in the building. Nobody here sells houses. Every hour we spend is spent on apartments.

The money

How we actually get paid.

It is the first thing most people want to know, so it goes near the top rather than in the fine print.

Apartment communities set aside a marketing budget to fill their units. They pay a referral fee to whoever sends them a resident who signs a lease. That fee is where all of our money comes from.

The renter never pays us. No fee, no deposit, no percentage. Your rent is not a dollar higher for using us either, because the fee comes out of the community's marketing budget and not out of your lease.

You get the apartment

The right fit, at the best price that community is offering, plus up to $250 cash back after you move in.

The community gets a resident

A filled unit is worth more to them than the fee they pay for it, which is why the budget exists in the first place.

Your agent gets paid for the match

Only after you sign. Nothing is owed by anyone if we send you somewhere you do not want to live.

The honest version of that

We only work with communities that pay a referral fee. That is what makes this free for you, and it does mean our list is not every apartment in Dallas. We are also not paid unless you actually sign, so steering you somewhere that does not fit what you asked for earns us nothing and costs us the repeat business that keeps the lights on.

Two more things worth saying out loud. We earn no commission from any moving company, so a mover quote we pass along is just a quote. We do earn a commission when you set up electricity, internet or renters insurance through our Connect Team. That service costs you nothing either way, but you should know where the money is before you decide.

Part of the referral fee comes back to you as up to $250 cash back, paid by Zelle, Venmo, PayPal or an Amazon gift card. See how the amounts work.

Fit

What we specialize in, and what we don't.

Knowing where we are useful is worth more to you than a page telling you we do everything. If we are the wrong call, we would rather you find that out in the first minute than the second week.

Where we are genuinely useful

This is the market we track daily and the work we have done for 23 years.

  • Apartments, and only apartments A licensed Texas brokerage that focuses 100% on apartment rentals, not home sales. We are not going to try to sell you a house on the way out.
  • Dallas and the northern suburbs The Dallas urban core plus Frisco, Allen, McKinney and Plano is where we know the inventory best. We cover the rest of DFW, including Fort Worth, but those are the areas we are in every day.
  • Pricing, specials and the parts nobody publishes Which floor plans are priced well right now, which communities have a real move-in special, and the things a listing never tells you about a building.
  • Renters who will be approved without friction Most of the communities we work with, especially the new ones, screen hard on income and credit. That is who we are set up to place quickly.

Where we are not the right resource

We will help anyone we can if you reach out, and Fair Housing applies to every person who contacts us without exception. But we do not have the community relationships to be much use in these situations, and pretending otherwise would waste your time.

  • Second-chance leasing Broken leases, evictions, or an approval problem on your record. Start here instead.
  • Housing vouchers and Section 8 We do not place voucher holders, and the communities on our list generally do not participate. Better places to look.
  • Short-term and furnished stays Anything under a standard lease term is a different market with different operators. Where to go for that.
  • Buying a home We are a rentals-only brokerage. If you are buying, you want a residential agent, and that is not us.

None of that is a judgment about anyone. It is just the difference between a company that can help you in a week and one that will string you along for a month.
