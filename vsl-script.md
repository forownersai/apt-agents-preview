# VSL Script — Apartment Agents (Dallas)
_Drafted 2026-08-04. Presenter: female Apartment Agents team member, core 25–35 demo. Placement: the page renters land on AFTER submitting for their deal info, before the full signup form._

**Runtime target:** ~2:50 at a natural conversational pace (≈440 words).
**Evergreen rule:** nothing in this script needs to change unless the reward changes. No deal number, no monthly volume figure, no employee-specific stat, no dated claim. See [[messaging-and-claims]] for why.

## BEAT 1 — HOOK (0:00–0:15)
_On camera. No overlay. Let her deliver this cold._

> Have you ever noticed that when you're searching on the big apartment websites, apartments pop up that aren't even in the area you were looking? And the same few places keep showing up no matter what you change?
>
> There's a reason for that.

## BEAT 2 — THE TWIST (0:15–0:55)
_This is the beat the whole video is built on. Her voice stays calm and matter-of-fact. The graphics do the accusing._

> Those websites don't work for you. You've never paid them a dollar. The apartments pay them, and what they're paying for is position.

**OVERLAY →** `apartments-com-hero.png` (hold 3s, URL visible)

> This is apartments.com's own sales page. Not my words. Theirs.

**OVERLAY →** `apartments-com-tiers-row.png` (hold 4s, then push in)

> Diamond. Platinum. Gold. Three hundred percent more exposure.

**OVERLAY →** `apartments-com-tiers-full.png`, punch in on **"Placement above Gold listings"** (hold 3s)

> Placement above Gold listings. That's a paid ladder, and you're the product being sold. The apartments that don't buy in are still out there. You just never see them.

## BEAT 3 — THE HIDDEN COST (0:55–1:25)
_Back on camera. Slow down here._

> And here's the part that actually costs you something. A listing will tell you the square footage and whether they take dogs. It won't tell you the walls are thin, or that maintenance takes two weeks to answer a request, or that management just changed hands, or that there's a bar behind the building that goes until two.
>
> You find that out after you've signed a twelve month lease.

## BEAT 4 — WHO WE ARE + THE MECHANISM (1:25–2:05)

> I'm [NAME] with Apartment Agents. We're a licensed Texas brokerage and we've done nothing but apartment rentals since 2003. Not home sales. Apartments.
>
> We keep our own database on the places we send people. Which management companies actually answer. Which buildings get noise complaints. Which ones are quietly dealing this week.
>
> Your licensed apartment expert scours the market for the best-priced floor plans and move-in specials, then sends you the ones that fit, with our top picks up front.

## BEAT 5 — THE STACK (2:05–2:25)
**OVERLAY →** Apartment List Builder screenshot (results view)

> You tour whatever you want, whenever it suits you. No agent tagging along.
>
> Tell us which moving companies you want quotes from and they come to us, so the movers never get your phone number or your email. And we'll get your utilities and internet set up before you move.

## BEAT 6 — THE MONEY (2:25–2:45)
**OVERLAY →** "Up to $250 cash back" title card

> When you lease, you get up to two hundred and fifty dollars cash back. That's the highest in Dallas, and if you find a locator offering more on the same apartment, we'll match it.
>
> Paid straight to you. Zelle, Venmo, PayPal, or an Amazon gift card.

## BEAT 7 — THE CATCH (2:45–3:00)

> People always ask what the catch is. There isn't one. You find a great apartment, the property gets a great resident, and they pay us for making the match. It never costs you a dollar more than going direct.

## BEAT 8 — PROOF + CTA (3:00–3:20)
**OVERLAY →** Google review cards sliding in, then the 4.9 star rating

> We've helped more than two hundred thousand DFW renters since 2003.
>
> Fill out the form below and let's find yours.

## Guardrails baked into this script
Checked line by line against [[messaging-and-claims]] and [[reward-terms]]:
- "up to two hundred and fifty," never a flat $250
- **match** it, not beat it
- no price-verification claim, no "hand-curated," no tour booking, no mention of advancing the rebate
- movers: no cut, quotes come to us, they never get your contact info
- utilities: the service is described, independence is never claimed (we do earn commission there)
- no em dashes, no exclamation marks, plain words over industry words
- no claim that the only apartments you see are the ones that paid — free listings exist
- nothing about communities holding cheap units off market, which stays off camera permanently
