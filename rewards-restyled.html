How you earn it

One thing to remember.

When a leasing office asks how you heard about them, the answer is Apartment Agents. That is the whole job.

1

Get your matches first

Tell us what you're after and your licensed apartment expert sends over the places that fit, with the best-priced floor plans and the real move-in specials up front.

2

Write us in when you tour and when you apply

Every community hands you a guest card at the door and asks the same question on the lease application: how did you learn about this property? Put Apartment Agents there both times.

If nobody hands you a card, just say it out loud to the leasing agent. It takes five seconds and it's what tells the community the referral came from us.

3

Tell us where you leased

Send us the community and your move-in date, or fill out the reward claim, and we'll take it from there.

Not sure whether you did it right?

Plenty of people can't remember what they wrote on a guest card weeks later, and it works out fine. Tell us where you leased either way and we'll sort it out on our end. Nothing here is a test you can fail.

Read this if you have a roommate

Everyone on the lease lists us. All of you.

This is the one that costs people money, and it's the easiest to avoid.

A community can only pay one locator per lease. If you list Apartment Agents and your roommate lists a different company, or a website, or a friend, the community has two sources pointing at the same lease. That can cancel the referral out entirely, and the reward goes away for everyone on the lease.

So before anyone tours or applies, send your roommates one text: we're using Apartment Agents, put that down as how we heard about the place. Partners, co-signers, anybody whose name goes on the application. One answer, from all of you.
