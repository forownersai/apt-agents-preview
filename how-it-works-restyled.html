The process

Three steps. Here is what is inside each one.

No fees, no contract, and no obligation to tour anything you don't like. You can stop at any point and it costs you nothing.

1

Step one

Tell us what you're looking for

Where you want to live, your budget, your move-in date and your must-haves. A quick call or text, not a twenty-question form.

What we ask you

  • ✓The areas you are considering, or what you need to be close to. Work, school, a highway, family.
  • ✓Your budget, and whether that number is rent on its own or rent plus utilities, parking and pet rent.
  • ✓Your move-in date, and how far it can move if a better price shows up a couple of weeks either side.
  • ✓Must-haves and deal-breakers. Pets and their weight, covered parking, washer and dryer in the unit, a room you can work in, ground floor or top floor.
  • ✓Anything that could affect approval. Income, credit, rental history, a broken lease. Worth saying up front instead of finding out after an application fee.

What happens on our side

  • ✓Your search goes to one licensed apartment expert who works this market every day. You deal with that person, not a rotating queue.
  • ✓They turn your must-haves into actual search criteria. Bed count, price ceiling, pet policy, square footage, submarket.
  • ✓If your budget and your must-have list don't meet in the middle, you hear that early rather than after two weeks of dead ends.
2

Step two

We send the right matches

Your licensed apartment expert scours the market for the best-priced floor plans and move-in specials, then sends you the ones that fit, with our top picks up front.

What we are looking at

  • ✓Live pricing on individual floor plans, not the "starting at" number a listing site shows for the cheapest unit in the building.
  • ✓Move-in specials and concessions running right now. Free weeks, waived admin and application fees, reduced deposits. These change week to week, which is most of the reason a locator is worth anything.
  • ✓Which of your must-haves each community actually has, so you are not reading twelve amenity pages to find out nobody allows your dog.
  • ✓What we know that a listing never says. Which buildings get noise complaints, which ones are slow on maintenance, what a block feels like at night.

What lands in your inbox

  • ✓A list of apartments that fit what you asked for, with our top picks at the top.
  • ✓The floor plan, the price we are seeing, and the special attached to it, so you can compare like for like.
  • ✓Nothing you have to answer. Reply with what you liked and what you didn't, and the next round comes back sharper.

Prices move. A community can change a floor plan's price overnight and pull a special with no notice, so treat what we send as the number we are seeing that day. If a place is on your shortlist, it is worth confirming the price when you tour.

3

Step three

Tour your favorites and get up to $250 on us

Visit the apartments whenever it suits you. No agent tagging along, no schedules to coordinate.

Touring on your own terms

  • ✓Go when you want to go. Tuesday evening after work, Saturday morning, two places in a row on a lunch break. Nobody is waiting on you.
  • ✓Many communities now offer self-guided tours. Others will walk you around when you show up. Call ahead if you want a specific floor plan opened.
  • ✓See something you hate, or something you love that wasn't on the list. Tell us either way and we adjust.

The part that protects your reward

  • ✓At every community you visit, list Apartment Agents on the guest card as how you learned about their property. Do it before you tour, not after.
  • ✓Put the same answer on your application when you apply.
  • ✓Applying with a roommate or a partner? Everyone on the lease gives the same answer. A community only pays one locator per lease, so a different name from one applicant can cancel the reward for all of you.
  • ✓Tell us where you leased and we get your reward started. It is paid straight to you by Zelle, Venmo, PayPal or an Amazon gift card. See the amounts.
After you pick a place

The rest of the move, handled.

Finding the apartment is the part everyone talks about. Then there is a moving truck to book and five utility accounts to open, usually in the same week. That part is on us too.

Mover quotes, gathered for you

You tell us which moving companies you want quotes from. We send them your move details and collect what comes back, so you are comparing real numbers side by side instead of chasing five companies yourself.

We don't take a cut from any of them. No referral links, no kickback baked into your price. A quote is just a quote, and you book whoever you want, direct.

And none of the sales calls

The quotes come to us, not to you. The movers never get your phone number or email, so you are not fielding calls and texts from five companies for the rest of the week.

Pick the one you want and we pass along your contact details to that company only.

Utilities on for move-in day

Our Connect Team compares rates on electricity, internet and renters insurance, then has everything set up and switched on for your move-in date. One conversation instead of calling around yourself, and it costs you nothing.

They will also tell you what the building requires. Plenty of communities want proof of renters insurance and power in your name before they hand over keys.

Straight answers

What we don't do.

Three things worth knowing before you start, so nothing about this comes as a surprise halfway through.

We don't escort tours

You tour on your own. We don't drive you around, we don't sit in the leasing office with you, and we don't book your appointments.

That is the trade. You get your evenings and weekends back instead of working around somebody else's calendar. If you want a person walking the halls with you, we are not that.

We don't charge renters

No fee, no deposit, no percentage, not at any point. Communities set aside a marketing budget to fill their units and pay us only when someone we refer signs a lease.

Your rent is not a dollar higher for using us. Going direct gets you the same apartment at the same price, minus the reward.

We don't work with communities paying under 50%

If a community pays less than 50% of a month's rent as a referral fee, it is not on the list we search. That does keep some buildings out of your results.

It is also what funds the cash back and the free service, and we would rather say so than pretend the entire market is on the table.
